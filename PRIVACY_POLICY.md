# Privacy Policy // The Guardian OS

**Effective Date: April 27, 2026**

The Guardian OS ("the Platform") is engineered with an "Absolute Zero" data retention philosophy. Your career intelligence is your private asset; we are merely the diagnostic engine that secures it.

## 1. Data Minimization Protocol
We do not store your personal identifiable information (PII) on our primary intelligence servers. 
- **Career Scans**: Your job titles, skills, and financial targets are processed in real-time to generate your Tactical Dossier.
- **Neural Processing**: All intelligence is processed via the Google Gemini 2.0 Flash API under secure, non-training protocols.

## 2. Localized Intelligence
The Guardian OS prioritizes local processing. Your geospatial data is used solely to tune the market pulse engine for your specific city-node and is never sold to third-party data brokers.

## 3. Zero-Retention PDF Generation
Once your Tactical Dossier (PDF) is generated and downloaded, the temporary session data is purged from our active memory buffers. We do not maintain a permanent database of your career roadmaps unless you explicitly opt-in for "Mission Tracking" services.

## 4. Encryption Standards
All data in transit is protected by AES-256 grade encryption. Your connection to the Guardian Command Center is secured via high-authority SSL/TLS protocols.

## 5. User Rights
As a operative of the Guardian OS, you have the absolute right to:
- Request the immediate purging of any active session data.
- Opt-out of non-essential diagnostic telemetry.

## 6. Policy Updates
This protocol may be upgraded as the AI automation landscape evolves. Operatives will be notified of major "System Hardening" updates via the Platform dashboard.
