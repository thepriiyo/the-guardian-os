The Guardian OS ("the Platform") is engineered with an **"Absolute Zero"** data retention philosophy. Your career intelligence is your private asset; we are merely the diagnostic engine that processes it for your survival.

## 1. DATA MINIMIZATION PROTOCOL
We do not store your personal identifiable information (PII) on our permanent intelligence servers.
- **Career Scans**: Your role data, skill matrix, and financial targets are processed in-memory during your active session.
- **Neural Processing**: All intelligence is processed via the **Google Gemma 3** neural engine using enterprise-grade, non-training protocols. Your data is never used to train future iterations of the model.

## 2. TEMPORAL GROUNDING & SEARCH
The **Market Pulse** engine utilizes real-time Google Search Grounding. Your search queries are anonymized before being transmitted to external search providers to ensure your tactical intent remains private.

## 3. ZERO-RETENTION DOSSIERS
Once your **Tactical Dossier (PDF)** is generated, all temporary session data is purged from our active memory buffers. The Platform does not maintain a repository of generated roadmaps; once downloaded, the intelligence is solely in your possession.

## 4. ENCRYPTION & SECURITY
All data in transit is protected by **AES-256** grade encryption. Your uplink to the Guardian Command Center is secured via high-authority SSL/TLS 1.3 protocols.

## 5. OPERATIVE RIGHTS
As an operative of the Guardian OS, you maintain:
- The right to immediate session termination and buffer purge.
- Total sovereignty over your generated PDF Dossier.
- Anonymity during the "Neural Scan" phase.

## 6. PROTOCOL EVOLUTION
This policy is updated as the automation landscape shifts. Major hardening updates will be logged in the System Status dashboard.
